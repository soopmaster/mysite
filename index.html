About Me

Hi! I'm António. I'm currently (but not for long!) taking my Electrotechnical Master's at FCT NOVA. My thesis is on drone control in adversarial defense scenarios, focusing on NMPC based methods and reachability analysis. Currently preparing my dissertation defense.

Beyond that I draw, I paint (watercolors), I do archery, I write a bit, make videogames occasionally, I travel when able. Film enthusiast, bookworm (especially for sci-fi), LEGO and model kit builder. This blog is to share some achievements and to present my work on my terms.
